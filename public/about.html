From accessories shop to dealership

Nairobi Powerbikes started in 2017 selling motorcycle accessories. Today we bring in Voge, Taro, Tekken and Zonsen bikes, run our own service centre and kit out riders from their first helmet to their third bike.

Riders selling to riders

Our team is made up of riders, mechanics and gear specialists. We'd rather help you pick the right first bike than the most expensive one.

Our aim is simple: make quality motorcycles affordable across East Africa, and build a riding community around them.
